BACKSPIN

Drum the pads, scratch the record, win the crowd.

On a touch screen the club is the controller: tap the pads (with as many fingers as you like), drag a finger up and down on the record, tap the fader, and tap the hype meter when it’s full. Hold a phone sideways; a tablet gives you the biggest pads.

Every sound is synthesized in your browser as the page loads: no samples, no audio files. How it’s made

How to play

Notes fly down the tunnel to eight pads. Hit a note’s pad as it reaches the pad’s frame. The bottom row is the drum kit and the top row is for the bigger moments. Every pad is an instrument: it always sounds when you hit it, and the song’s own hits for your part only play when you play them.

A S D F Kick, snare, open hat, clap (the bottom row)
Q W E R Laser, crash, vox, horn (the top row)
J K L ; and U…P The same pads under your right hand
Hold mouse + move Scratch: up plays forward, down plays back
Z X C Crossfader left, centre, right
Space Hype Boost, when the meter is full
1 2 3 Switch loops (freestyle and sandbox)
4 The song’s own pad hits on or off (sandbox)
Esc Pause

Round notes come down the left lane to the record. Hold the mouse button and move the mouse the way the arrows point for as long as the note lasts. A bar under the pads is a crossfader cut: press Z, X or C as it lands.

Beat pads. Plug in a MIDI pad controller and connect it in Settings. Its first eight pads are the game’s eight, in the same places (notes 36 to 43, bottom row first), and how hard you hit sets how loud the pad plays. Any pad can be taught another note.

Mouse, Hybrid, touch. The game starts on whatever suits your device, and you can change it when you pick a level. With Mouse, the mouse plays it all: click the pads, hold the record and drag it up and down, click or slide the fader, click the hype meter to boost, and ❚❚ pauses. One pointer can’t be in two places, so notes that land together or too close to reach are left to the song. Hybrid plays six pads: A, S and D under your other hand for the kick, snare and clap, and the mouse for the crash, vox and horn above them. On a touch screen it’s all taps, with every finger you’ve got.

Practice. Pick a section and loop it at 60, 75 or 90% speed. Your part plays quietly underneath as a guide, every hit shows how many milliseconds early or late it was, and the results show your timing pad by pad.

Good hits build the crowd’s hype. When the meter is full, Space doubles your score for eight beats. In the freestyle there are no notes at all: drum, scratch in time, switch loops, cut the crossfader, mix it up, and don’t let the music sit there on its own.
