Pitline: a race team simulator

A 47-second film of a toy race circuit that runs itself, with a race team's app over it. Car 9 gets a strategy call to pit a lap early, changes tyres in 2.4 seconds, and comes out ahead of car 3 when car 3 pits a lap later. On the way the view visits the team's wind tunnel. Space pauses, the arrow keys skip 2 seconds, and keys 1 to 4 jump to the chapters. Read how it was built.
